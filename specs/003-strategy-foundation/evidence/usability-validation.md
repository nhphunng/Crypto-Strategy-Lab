## Protocol

Recruit at least five users familiar with basic trading concepts but not the implementation. Give each participant one built-in strategy and two generated drafts (one passing and one validation-failed), without coaching beyond the UI disclaimer.

Record whether each participant can:

1. identify exact strategy/version, parameter defaults and ranges, dataset provenance, signal timestamp/action/phase, and warm-up meaning (SC-011);
2. explain how to add another compliant strategy using metadata/registration without naming MA or RSI in downstream consumers (SC-012);
3. distinguish extracted rules, evidence, explicit assumptions, validation findings, fingerprints, and the effect of confirmation before activation (SC-020).

Pass thresholds are those stated in `spec.md`. Record anonymized participant ID, task outcome, incorrect interpretation, time, and follow-up comment. Do not record prompts, raw protected source content, credentials, or personal data.
