## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored FOR this product.** High specificity — not a category-interchangeable dashboard. Vocabulary is the product's (`timeframe`, `candle`, `backtest`, `drawdown`, `Sharpe`, `profit factor`, `position sizing`, `seed`, `Top-K`, `scoring policy`, `versioned strategy`). Product-specific artifacts that only make sense here: the Provenance drawer (`BT-1846 · immutable record`, dataset checksum, `Scoring policy Balanced v2`, `Seed 424242`), the seeded Random Search worker, Dependency Health panel, and `--color-ml` (#A78BFA) reserved for ML "score" numerics. The Landing scaffold is the generic seam (`Open Strategy Lab` CTA, "Built for analysts, not gamblers").

**Tension:** specificity becomes insular in the Provenance drawer and Operations (`id@version`, checksums, `worker`, `candidateLimit`, `projection v`) — high specificity and low approachability collide.

## Overall Impression

A high-craft, high-specificity quant-research workspace whose copy is unusually honest ("no real trades, no funds, not a forecast") and whose pedagogy layer is genuinely product-authored. The flaws are not about craft — they are about **trust**: a handful of affordances look live but do nothing, a headline configuration panel is read-only (so it *looks* configurable but isn't), and a subtle accessibility contrast drift on dim labels sits just under the AA line.

## What's Working

1. **The explanations system is product-authored, not template.** `Show explanations` drives InfoNote / HelperText / LearnTooltip / RecoBadge / WhyLink; every indicator has a human `purpose` string; a post-backtest "What happened?" paragraph narrates the result. Converts a jargon-heavy domain into a ramp while leaving advanced controls on.
2. **Honest, non-hyping consequence copy everywhere.** "No real trades, no funds, no financial advice," "not a prediction," "one historical test, not a forecast." Restraint is the top trust engine in a finance tool.
3. **A real reproducibility artifact.** The Provenance drawer (dataset ID, checksum, seed, policy id@version, immutable rules) turns the Landing's "deterministic backtests · reproducible results" claim into a concrete, openable UI object. That's a differentiator expressed as design.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Dead / cosmetic controls.** Shell.tsx TopBar `Search ⌘K` (no onClick) and `Reconnect` IconBtn; Market.tsx PageHeader `Reconnect` + `Fullscreen workspace` + ChartPane `More`; the `Sync crosshair` toggle sets a `sync` state nothing reads; News `Simulate degraded`/`Restore sentiment`. **Why:** an Operate tool's contract is that affordances act; a control that does nothing erodes trust in the data, and an advertised `⌘K` that doesn't open is an explicit broken promise. **Fix:** wire Search into a real command palette + `Ctrl/Cmd+K`; give Reconnect a real reconnect sequence + toast; remove or hide Fullscreen/More until implemented; drop Sync crosshair or implement per-pane sync; gate the News demo toggle behind a dev flag.
- **[P1] A headline feature you can't configure.** Strategy Search's `Search Configuration` panel (Backtests.tsx StrategySearch, ~833–859) shows Market, Timeframe, the four `Strategies to combine` chips, `Combination size "2–4"`, and `Candidates to try` — all read-only (chips are `<span>`s). Only `Basic/Advanced` and `Start/Stop` are live. **Why:** this is the primary decision surface for a seeded Random Search — a power user must drive it, and the chips look clickable. **Fix:** make strategies a togglable multi-select, combination size a Segmented (2/3/4), candidate limit editable, then feed the real config into `startSearch`.
- **[P1] WCAG low-contrast cluster (detector-only catch).** 18 findings: `#687586` (faint/dim) on `#0b0e13` 4.1:1, on `#131820` 3.8:1, on `#1d2632` 3.3:1; `#ffffff` on `#4f7cff` 3.7:1 (blue CTAs). **Why:** dim label text at 3.3–4.1:1 fails AA for body-size text; separate from the (possibly acceptable) button-text portion. A code-only review under-weights this. **Fix:** lift `--color-dim`/`--color-faint` toward `#a5b2c2`+ or raise contrast on the surfaces they're used on; keep white-on-accent for large/heading text only.
- **[P1] Two-product identity clash + static preview numbers.** The Landing (44px hero, glows, scroll-reveal) and the Operate shell (13–15px terminal) share tokens but not register; the walkthrough `StepPreview` hardcodes `63,008.57 / +24.2% / -7.1%` while `ProductPreview` reads live mock values. **Why:** the brand fracts at the seam, and a reproducibility-themed product showing frozen numbers undercuts its own thesis. **Fix:** carry the eyebrow-gradient/tabular ticker into the app; feed live values into previews or label them "Illustrative."
- **[P2] Explain-by-default is backwards for the primary persona.** `showExplain` defaults ON (`store.tsx`: `getItem('csl.showExplain') !== '0'`). **Why:** the analyst/power user gets InfoNotes, HelperText and RecoBadges everywhere out of the box — noise for someone who already knows the domain. **Fix:** default off (or first-run "on" then respect dismissal); cover the Operations/Provenance jargon the explain layer bypasses.
- **[P3] No tablet/mobile treatment for the app shell.** `AppShell` is `h-screen` with a fixed-width nav rail; `WatchlistPanel` is fixed `w-[210px]`; only the search grid reflows below 1100px. **Why:** the product promises "no account, no install" and the Landing is responsive — but the tool is desktop-only. **Fix:** collapse the rail to an icon sheet on small widths, stack the watchlist under the charts, allow density reduction / horizontal scroll.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (power user):** `⌘K` advertised but dead; no keyboard shortcuts or command palette; explain-layer infobands on by default; the Search config he'd drive is read-only; rank/score controls buried in selects.
- **Jordan (first-timer):** the ramp is strong, but the Provenance drawer and Operations screen ignore the explain layer (`id@version`, checksums, workers, Generated/Queued/Failed/Retried tiles), and "Strategies to combine" chips look clickable but aren't (click → nothing).
- **Sam (accessibility):** strong base (`:focus-visible` ring, `prefers-reduced-motion`, `role="dialog"` + focus-trap + Escape, `aria-live` toasts, `role="switch"`, `aria-pressed` segmented, SignalTag / StatusBadge always icon+label). Red flags: Landing capability-card "Open" affordance is `opacity-0` until hover (invisible to keyboard/mobile); TopBar `Show explanations` label is `hidden sm:inline` so the switch is an unlabeled track under `sm`; the whole page wraps in an inner scroll container that can complicate screen-reader scroll discovery.
- **Riley (stress-tester):** strongest area — Live/Reconnecting/Stale status, backfill progress, dependency degradation handled with reassurance, Stop-flows confirm + "preserved". Red flags: the topbar `ConnectionStatus` is a demo state toggle (`title` says "toggle demo connection state") — can't tell real from test; News "Simulate degraded" breaks state with only a manual "Restore".
- **Casey (mobile):** essentially unaddressed in Operate mode (see P3).

## Minor Observations

- Dead `LegacySingleBacktest` ships in Backtests.tsx (hardcoded `+24.2% / 62% / -7.1%`, fake 1.2s run) alongside the live API `SingleBacktest` — same screen, two result sources; delete the legacy screen.
- Font-size tokens don't exist: ad-hoc `text-[10px]` vs `text-[11px]` uppercase, plus stray `text-[11.5px]`/`text-[13.5px]`. Caption typography drifts across screens.
- Toast storm risk: frequent actions each fire a toast; the Strategy wizard fires save/hydrate/prefill consecutively.
- Partial persistence: `market`, `watchlist`, `showExplain` persist to localStorage, but `timeframe`, `activeStrategy`, layout, backtest params, and search/loop configs do not.

## Questions to Consider

1. If this is "reproducible evidence," why does the walkthrough show frozen `+24.2% / -7.1% / 63,008.57` while the tool computes fresh values?
2. The Explain toggle is the whole pedagogy and defaults ON — so is this a first-time product wearing an expert tool's clothes, or an expert tool afraid of its own jargon?
3. "Sync crosshair" syncs nothing, `⌘K` searches nothing, "Strategies to combine" can't be changed — what does a single broken affordance cost a product whose core promise is trust?
